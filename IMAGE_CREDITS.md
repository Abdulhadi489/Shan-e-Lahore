The four page banners (About, Contact, Terms, Privacy) are photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, credit appreciated). Each was cropped to 1600 x 450 px and compressed to WebP; nothing else was changed.

To replace one, save a new file with the same name in `src/assets/banners/` (about 1600 px wide, wide crop, under about 250 KB) and update its line here. The page shows a warm dark-orange tint over the photo, so any warm food or restaurant photo works.
